Community Guidelines

  • Hobbiyaa is for finding hobby partners — not a dating app. Keep interactions about the shared activity.
  • Meet in public places for your first meetup with someone new.
  • Be respectful in posts, comments, and chats. Harassment of any kind isn't tolerated.
  • Only post real, genuine plans — no spam or misleading events.
  • Report anything that makes you uncomfortable, and use Block if needed — it's immediate and mutual.

Need help?

Having trouble with your account, a post, or someone's behavior? Reach out and we'll help sort it out.

Email: support@hobbiyaa.app

For urgent safety concerns, use the Report or Block option directly on the person's profile or post first — it takes effect immediately.
